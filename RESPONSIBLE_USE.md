# Responsible Use Policy

OCULUS reads vehicle licence plates from video and keeps a record of where and when each vehicle was seen. Software like this can be used to follow, profile or intimidate people. That's why it is published only so people can see the author's work, and its use is limited to a few narrow purposes.

> This policy explains the [LICENSE](LICENSE), which is what actually applies. This document is not legal advice. **You** are responsible for complying with the law wherever you run the software.

## The only permitted uses

- **Viewing**: reading the code, for example on GitHub.
- **Evaluation**: instructors, examiners, reviewers and prospective employers or collaborators running it to evaluate the author's work.
- **Personal learning**: running and modifying a private copy for your own non-commercial study, using footage you recorded yourself or are otherwise allowed to use.

## Forbidden

Everything else is forbidden. In particular:

- redistributing the software or publishing copies of it
- commercial use
- deploying it on real camera feeds or streams
- using it, or data it produces, in another project, product, service or publication

## Never, under any circumstances

Even for viewing, evaluation or personal learning, you must never use OCULUS or data it produces to:

1. **Stalk, follow, track or monitor a specific person** or their vehicle.
2. **Harass, intimidate, threaten, blackmail or harm** anyone.
3. **Build profiles of individuals**, such as their movements, habits, home or work locations, or the people they meet.
4. Carry out **covert or mass surveillance** of any place.
5. **Discriminate** against people, or target them because of their ethnicity, religion, political opinion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or any other protected characteristic. This includes targeting journalists, activists, protesters or minorities.
6. **Sell, share or publish** plate sightings or movement data tied to identifiable people, or combine them with other data to identify people.
7. Act in any way that **violates privacy or data-protection law**. This includes KVKK (Turkey, Law No. 6698), the GDPR (EU) and their equivalents.

Breaking any of these rules ends all your rights under the licence.

## When you evaluate or study it

- **Collect the minimum.** Delete sightings, crops and video once you no longer need them.
- **Keep data local.** OCULUS processes everything on your machine and sends nothing to the cloud.
- **Keep a human in the loop.** Readings can be wrong. Never act against a person based on an automatic reading.
- **Use mock data only.** The registry uses generated (fake) owner data. Never fill it with real personal data.

OCULUS is provided "as is", without any warranty. Plate readings can be wrong. The author is not liable for any use or misuse of the software or of the data it produces.

If you see this project being used against people, open an issue in the repository or contact the author.
